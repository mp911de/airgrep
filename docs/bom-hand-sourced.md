## Through-hole parts on the carrier (hand-soldered)

D2, D3, J1 to J6, J8 to J13, JP1, JP3 and SW4 are marked DNP, so the assembly BOM and position files leave them out. Their footprints are on the board; fit the parts below by hand. R90/R91 are separate DNP pull-up pads for bench tuning. The mounting holes (MH1 to MH4, MH6 to MH8, MH12 to MH14) are board-only footprints and are excluded from the assembly files as well.
