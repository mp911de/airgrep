## Startup, shutdown and layout qualifications

- **OK:** No MCU is required for normal enable, reverse blocking, OVLO or current protection. Q12 disables the PMID display feed when SW_EN is high. With USB absent its source vanishes; U12 can power the shared display output without intentionally back-powering PMID. U11 independently supplies ADSBee.
- **OK:** Overvoltage recovery is automatic only after OVLO falls below its falling threshold; the L suffix does not latch OVLO itself. Overcurrent/overtemperature reset requires VIN to reach 0 V or EN below VSD, not merely below the UVLO threshold. [TI §7.3.3, §7.3.8/9](../../datasheets/tps25947.pdf#page=38).
- **WARN:** D10's documented ≤0.715 V forward-drop reference is below the 1.076 V minimum falling UVLO threshold and can disable U14 during QON reset. It does not establish EN <0.45 V guaranteed shutdown/reset threshold. Treat QON reset and clearing a latched U14 fault as different operations; verify Q12-low and actual VIN discharge in recovery tests. [TI §6.5 p9, §7.3.9 p38](../../datasheets/tps25947.pdf#page=9).
- **WARN:** R72's longer multilayer ILM route and shared GND deserve quiet-node inspection. TI recommends ILM parasitic capacitance below 50 pF and separation from switching signals; extracted capacitance/coupling is unavailable. This is a verification gap, not a proven oscillation. [TI §8.4.1 p63](../../datasheets/tps25947.pdf#page=63).
- **WARN:** No local output Schottky was found in these feed networks. Its necessity depends on interruption current and loop inductance. OUT must remain above -0.3 V, except the stated <1 µs -0.8 V pulse allowance; measure rather than infer compliance from ERC/DRC. [TI §6.1, §8.3.1](../../datasheets/tps25947.pdf#page=7).
