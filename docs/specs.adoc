== Technical data: airgrep carrier

=== Product

Portable traffic-awareness receiver for gliders and general aviation. It receives ADS-B on 1090 MHz, takes its position from GNSS and shows traffic on a 1.47 inch touch display.

It is an awareness aid, not certified avionics. It must not be the primary means of collision avoidance or navigation. FLARM (868 MHz) reception remains a hardware-validation item. software progress is tracked in link:firmware-plan.md[firmware-plan.md].

=== Requirements

==== Function

. Receive 1090 MHz ADS-B and 868 MHz FLARM, receive only, with an ADSBee 1090U.
. GNSS with Galileo and PPS: u-blox MAX-M10S on the carrier, UART and PPS to the ADSBee.
. Local display: gets traffic and status from the ADSBee over a direct UART and the battery state of charge over I2C.
. Wi-Fi stays available as an alternative link to the display. A maintained Wi-Fi slider controls the ADSBee radio, primarily for the EFB feed and to save battery power. The ESP applies its position through AT commands on the existing UART in the custom ADSBee firmware.
. Wi-Fi feed to an EFB (GDL90).
. Quiet piezo feedback for ground use, with a maintained MUTE slider.
. Low battery does not override the Wi-Fi slider. Show a low-battery warning, automatic backlight dimming may remain.

==== Power

. Battery: 1S LiPo pack, 3.7 V, with built-in protection, behind the PCB. The reference pack is 10 Ah, 100 x 60 x 11 mm.
. Load: 5 V at 0.8 A nominal and 1 A expected peak. Up to 1.5 A is short-term headroom, not a continuous requirement. Peak duration and repetition are still to be measured.
. Charging through a carrier-mounted USB-C receptacle at 5 V only. The device never requests a higher voltage. Overvoltage on the input is cut off in hardware.
. The USB source supplies what it can and the battery supplies the remainder. Sources: Type-C at 1.5 A or 3 A, USB-A chargers at 1 to 2.4 A through an A-to-C cable, PC ports at 0.5 A.
. Fast charge at 1.8 A nominal. With the device on, the cell gets what remains after the system load.

==== Manufacturing

. JLCPCB fabricates the board and assembles all SMD parts. The user solders module sockets and harness connectors.
. Modules stay removable. A shunt per 5 V feed lets a module stay unpowered in its socket.
. Antennas: SMA bulkhead connections in the enclosure for GNSS, 1090 MHz and sub-GHz.
. Enclosure: custom 3D print, designed after the PCB.

=== Design decisions

The active target is `hardware/carrier-ti-charger/`.

==== Charger and power path

* FUSB303B supplies CC advertisement over the shared I2C bus. BC1.2 remains in the charger.
* TPS61088 and TPS22975 provide a switched 5 V receiver rail. A disabled boost still conducts, so U7 is required. The selected inductors and timing components are in the schematic; candidate integrated-disconnect boosts are not fitted designs.
* TPS259474L feeds isolate the modules, block reverse current and cut overvoltage. Fault recovery may require cycling the affected input.
* POWER controls enable signals, not battery current. With USB present, the display remains available in charger mode while the receiver rail is off.

==== Independent protection and recovery

* The pack NTC controls charging through TS without firmware. A separate thermostat disables charging through CE and removes receiver power.
* TLV840 monitors SYS so USB-only operation remains possible. The undervoltage latch prevents repeated restarts when the unloaded pack voltage rebounds. POWER or USB clears it.
* Q13 and SW4 provide a QON system reset without opening the case.

==== Interfaces and mechanics

* Wired UART keeps traffic and Wi-Fi control independent of the radio link.
* MAX-M10S is soldered on the carrier.
* Four copper layers provide a ground reference, power distribution and heat spreading.

==== Low-battery cut-off voltage

The supervisor measures SYS on the board, not the cell. Under load, SYS sits below the cell's open-circuit voltage by the drop across pack, harness, fuse and charger switch. So the cut-off seen at the cell depends on the load:

==== Limits of the operating range

The operating range is a target bounded by component ratings, not an assembled-device qualification. The thermal model assumes a 115 x 105 x 40 mm case, four 8 cm² vents, 50 % open grilles and 35 mm vertical separation. The case is not designed yet.

link:../hardware/sim/heat/ti/results.md[TI heat results] describe the recorded PCB snapshot. Charging while operating can reach the TS stop. high ambient temperature can reach the thermostat trip. Validate cooling, pack temperature and charging in the actual enclosure. Old MAX-board results do not describe the active board.

==== Usage matrix and runtime

Runtime from a full charge to the supervisor cut-off, with 95 % of the rated capacity usable. The aged column assumes 80 % remaining capacity. The display's Wi-Fi is off in all cases. the display talks to the ADSBee over UART.
