# Technical data: Flybee carrier

Status 25 Sep 2026. Engineering data for the carrier board with its modules, written for end users, resellers and ourselves. It follows the structure the EU rules expect (radio, charging, battery, safety information) but is not a declaration of conformity. Values marked *estimate* still need a bench measurement.

The power and runtime sections are generated by `tools/power_budget.py` from `specs/power_params.json`. Edit the parameters and re-run the script; do not edit the generated block by hand.

## Product

Portable traffic-awareness receiver for gliders and general aviation. It receives ADS-B on 1090 MHz, determines its own position by GNSS and shows traffic on a 1.47 inch touch display. It is an awareness aid, not certified avionics, and must not be used as the primary means of collision avoidance or navigation. FLARM (868 MHz) reception is not yet supported by the receiver firmware.

## Radio equipment

| Function | Module | Band | Direction |
|---|---|---|---|
| ADS-B | ADSBee 1090U | 1090 MHz | receive only |
| Sub-GHz (UAT 978 MHz / 868 MHz) | ADSBee 1090U, TI CC1312 | 868 / 978 MHz | receive only in this product (hardware can transmit; firmware does not) |
| GNSS | u-blox MAX-M10S | L1 / E1 / B1 / G1, 1559 to 1606 MHz | receive only |
| Wi-Fi (traffic output to EFB apps, configuration) | ADSBee ESP32-S3 | 2.4 GHz, IEEE 802.11 b/g/n | transmit and receive, switchable |
| Wi-Fi / Bluetooth LE | Display ESP32-S3 | 2.4 GHz | disabled by firmware in normal use |

Maximum transmit power and the module-level radio test reports (EN 300 328) must be taken from the module vendors' certificates before sale; both ESP32-S3 modules are pre-certified parts, the combination still needs the product-level assessment.

### Accuracy of these figures

- The ADSBee currents are the vendor's quick-spec values; the Wi-Fi value is an average with the access point active and will vary with traffic and client count.
- The display figures (MCU, panel, backlight) are estimates. Seeed publishes no current for this board. A bench measurement of the display alone at 5 V is the most valuable next step, followed by one full-system measurement per usage scenario.
- Early project notes assumed 0.8 to 1 A at 5 V. The datasheet-based model gives 0.23 A (Wi-Fi off) to 0.51 A (Wi-Fi on); runtimes quoted before 25 Sep that used 0.8 to 1 A are superseded.
- GNSS reception quality barely matters: continuous acquisition without a fix costs about 2 mA more than tracking.

## Safety information for users

- Charge only at room temperature; the device refuses to charge below 0 °C and above about 43 °C at the battery.
- Do not operate with a swollen, damaged or wet battery. Replace it with one of the qualified packs only, observing the polarity marking.
- Do not cover the device in direct sunlight; above 60 °C at the battery the thermostat switches it off.
- The traffic display is a situational-awareness aid. Keep a visual lookout.
