## Control and assumptions

Full 1.8 A is permitted while cool. A TMP300-like switch disables charging through THM at its trip point, then permits it again at trip minus 5 or 10 °C. The existing nominal pack JEITA stop/restart at 42.1/40.8 °C takes priority. Native U4 foldback at 130 °C remains active. USB keeps supplying the system when charging is disabled. This is a proposed control model, not a circuit or PCB change. Stop/resume operation of MAX77757 through THM still needs a bench check.

The local PCB node is used as the TMP300 sensor location with an assumed 2 s first-order lag. It is not the U4 junction. Exact placement, thermal coupling and sensor delay are unmeasured. The sensor has negligible added heating/capacity in this reduced network. Trip and hysteresis comparisons are evaluated every 0.25 s; turn-off is immediate and current rises with the existing assumed 5 s response when enabled. No separate minimum on/off time is imposed.

Other assumptions are unchanged from [charging.md](charging.md): fixed 3.7 V cell, USB 5 V / 3 A, 0.8 A system load, existing front sink and 5 cm² inlet/outlet, estimated thermal masses. No SOC evolution, CV taper, charger timer, NTC attachment lag, or battery ageing model. Curves end at 55 °C pack, the earliest thermostat trip. All current averaging is for display only; losses and I²R use instantaneous current. A fresh baseline is calibrated from the current board, so old artifacts may reflect a different board revision.

## TMP300B details

[TI datasheet, Rev F](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmp300.pdf): resistor-programmable trip, 5/10 °C hysteresis, open-drain output. B-grade total trip accuracy is ±6 °C maximum, ±2 °C typical; RSET tolerance adds to this. For all four thresholds, assume a 3.3 V sensor supply. Below 2.35 V the upper measurement limit is 100 × (VS - 0.95) °C, only 85 °C at 1.8 V. VTEMP is high impedance (210 kΩ); reading it requires care not to shift the comparator threshold. JLCPCB stock was not verified.

## Validation

Reduced steady model agrees with its board calibration within 5.68e-14 K. Maximum main-run energy residual is 4.93e-12 J per step. Unit tests cover hysteresis memory, immediate stop, restart, pack precedence, thermal lag, energy conservation and timestep convergence. These are numerical checks, not physical validation.
