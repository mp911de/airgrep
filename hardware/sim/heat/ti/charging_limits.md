## Control and assumptions

Host mode: the ESP32-S3 writes ICHG 1.8 A and the TREG register (60/80/100/120 °C) and pets the watchdog. Default mode: 1 A and TREG 120 °C, what the chip does after power-on or when the watchdog expires. The charger holds the junction at TREG with its analog loop (zero current 2 K above TREG in this model). The nominal pack JEITA stop/restart at 42.1/40.8 °C takes priority. USB keeps supplying the system when charging is reduced.

Other assumptions as in [charging.md](charging.md): fixed 3.7 V cell, USB 5 V / 3 A, 0.8 A system load, bottom plate, 5 cm² inlet/outlet, estimated thermal masses, unrouted copper paths assumed where the board has none yet. No SOC evolution, CV taper, charger timer or battery ageing.
