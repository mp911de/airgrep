# Charging with battery thermal inertia

Generated by `hardware/sim/run_charge_heat.py --write`. [Four-page PDF](charging.pdf). SVGs: [5 Ah](charging_5000.svg), [10 Ah](charging_10000.svg), [delay sensitivity](charging_delay.svg), [external spreader](charging_spreader.svg).

## Model

Five energy-conserving nodes: U4 junction, local PCB, top heatsink (unused with a bottom plate, which the local PCB node then includes), enclosure surroundings, pack. Thermal resistances and interpolated losses come from seven current points on the current board's high-corner steady-state model. The enclosure node includes air and the thermally participating case, main board and modules; it is not air alone. The pack has its own heat capacity and I²R heating. It heats through finite thermal resistance to the enclosure surroundings.

The 31.4 °C JEITA warm state lowers termination to 4.05 V, not charge current. At this fixed 3.7 V cell operating point the model remains below that voltage. No state-of-charge curve, constant-voltage taper, ageing or charge-completion prediction. The pack NTC is assumed to track pack temperature exactly; real attachment adds delay. The pack is one average temperature, not a core/surface model. Losses are frozen at the steady calibration points, so cold-start losses can be overestimated. The 35 °C runs use the same loss table. These are scenarios, not guaranteed bounds.

The delay page halves/doubles all heat capacities, and separately adds a 100 K/W PCB-to-pack path for gap/radiation/insulator coupling. These are sensitivity assumptions, not confidence intervals. The spreader page adds a 20 or 10 K/W local PCB-to-outside path. This includes interface, lateral spreading and external heat rejection. No selected product is claimed to achieve either value.

Charging stops before the old sustained-charge steady temperatures occur. At this load, module heat keeps warming the pack after JEITA suspends charging. The larger pack delays this; it does not remove the steady enclosure heat.

## Verification

Reduced steady U4/enclosure temperatures match the seven board-model calibration points within 5.68e-14 K. Maximum per-step energy residual across the main scenarios: 4.83e-12 J. Analytic exponential, energy conservation, JEITA hysteresis and timestep tests are in `test_charge_heat.py`. These establish numerical consistency, not physical accuracy.
