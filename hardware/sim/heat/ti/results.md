## Thermal environment

- Open bench: board in still air, 10 W/m²K on both faces (convection plus radiation), no enclosure.
- Closed case (not yet designed; assumed): 115 x 105 x 40 mm outside, 2 mm printed wall (k 0.2 W/m·K), 7 W/m²K inside and 9 W/m²K outside. Wall path to ambient 6.3 K/W over 418 cm². Inside it the board loses 6 W/m²K per face to the case air (modules above, battery below). The air carries everything dissipated in the case: board losses, the modules' 5 V power and the pack's own I²R. The pack: 9.2 K/W to the case air.
- Vents: 2 lower inlets and 2 upper outlets, 8.0 cm² each (16.0 cm² per group), 50 % open grille, 35 mm vertical separation, discharge coefficient 0.6. Natural draft acts in parallel with the wall path; no fan.
- Junction to board: BQ25798 RθJB 9.7 K/W from junction to pins (RθJC(top) 20.9, ψJT 0.5 K/W), TPS61088 4.1 K/W, TPS22975 RθJC(bot) 16.4 K/W; inductor winding to terminals 12 K/W.
- U4 heatsink: 10 x 10 mm aluminium sink (Fischer ICK S 10 x 10 x 6.5) on a 1.5 mm copper spacer, thermal epoxy. Junction to package top 21 K/W, epoxy and spacer 20 K/W, sink to air 35 K/W on the bench and 58 K/W in the case air. That gives 76 and 99 K/W from the junction, in parallel with the pins. Parts with pads inside the 10 x 10 mm footprint of the sink: C42, C43, C44, C46, C47, C6, R58, R59. The spacer must lift the sink base above the tallest of them.

## Limits of the model

- Steady state only. The 1.5 A output margin is short-term headroom and not a thermal case.
- The enclosure is not designed. Its size, material, vents and the battery position decide the case-air rise, which dominates the absolute temperatures in the closed case. The modules' 5 V power is most of the heat in it.
- Board-to-air coefficients are lumped (one value per face). Modules on the top side shade the board; their own heat reaches the board only through the case air, not through the header pins.
- The BQ25798 switching loss is fitted to the data sheet efficiency curve at 5 V in and 4 V out (read off the figure, about half a point of accuracy), so it carries the data sheet's test board and inductor.
- BQ25798: RθJB 9.7 K/W from junction to pins (RθJC(top) 20.9, ψJT 0.5 K/W).
- The U4 heatsink path is an estimate on top of that: junction to package top is not published for the FC2QFN (25 to 45 K/W assumed), the epoxy layers depend on the assembly, and the sink rating is a free-air figure derated for the lower temperature rise and the case air. Bench item: thermocouple on the sink base.
- Contact, fuse and pack resistances are data sheet maxima or estimates (see the input table). The USB cable is not included; its drop lowers CHGIN towards the 4.5 V input regulation threshold.
- Only DC currents are solved in the copper. Ripple currents in the capacitor loops and their ESR losses are small and stay inside the converter footprints.
- Bench verification: thermocouple or IR camera on U4, U6, L1, L2 and the pack at the charging case with the real enclosure, and at the 3.1 V / 0.8 A battery case.
