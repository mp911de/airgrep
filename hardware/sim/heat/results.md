## Thermal environment

- Open bench: board in still air, 10 W/m²K on both faces (convection plus radiation), no enclosure.
- Closed case (not yet designed; assumed): 115 x 105 x 40 mm outside, 2 mm printed wall (k 0.2 W/m·K), no vents, 7 W/m²K inside and 9 W/m²K outside. Case to ambient 6.3 K/W over 418 cm². Inside it the board loses 6 W/m²K per face to the case air (modules above, battery below). The air carries everything dissipated in the case: board losses, the modules' 5 V power and the pack's own I²R. The pack: 9.2 K/W to the case air.
- Junction to board: MAX77757 θJC 7.5 K/W onto its pins, TPS61088 RθJC(bot) 3.1 K/W plus 1 K/W solder onto its pad, TPS22975 RθJC(bot) 16.4 K/W; inductor winding to terminals 12 K/W.
- U4 heatsink: 10 x 10 mm aluminium sink (Fischer ICK S 10 x 10 x 6.5) on a 1.5 mm copper spacer, thermal epoxy. Junction to package top 35 K/W, epoxy and spacer 20 K/W, sink to air 35 K/W on the bench and 58 K/W in the case air. That gives 90 and 113 K/W from the junction, in parallel with the pins. Parts with pads inside the 10 x 10 mm footprint of the sink: C10, C11, C34, C5, C6, C7, R6. The spacer must lift the sink base above the tallest of them.

## Charging and the pack temperature window

The charger reads the pack NTC. Above 31.4 °C it lowers the termination voltage to 4.05 V, above 42.1 °C it stops (nominal thresholds, docs/power.md). In the closed case the pack sits in the case air, so the ambient at which these are reached is:

## Limits of the model

- Steady state only. The 1.5 A output margin is short-term headroom and not a thermal case.
- The enclosure is not designed. Its size, material, vents and the battery position decide the case-air rise, which dominates the absolute temperatures in the closed case. The modules' 5 V power is most of the heat in it.
- Board-to-air coefficients are lumped (one value per face). Modules on the top side shade the board; their own heat reaches the board only through the case air, not through the header pins.
- The MAX77757 has no published efficiency curve. Its switching loss uses the edge coefficient fitted to the TPS61088, scaled with voltage, current and frequency. The fit itself rests on the project's curve, which is TI's Figure 6-2 less an estimated 2 points.
- θJC values are JEDEC figures. For the flip-chip MAX77757 (no exposed pad) the 7.5 K/W is applied from the junction to its pins as a group.
- The U4 heatsink path is an estimate on top of that: junction to package top is not published for the FC2QFN (25 to 45 K/W assumed), the epoxy layers depend on the assembly, and the sink rating is a free-air figure derated for the lower temperature rise and the case air. Bench item: thermocouple on the sink base.
- Contact, fuse and pack resistances are data sheet maxima or estimates (see the input table). The USB cable is not included; its drop lowers CHGIN towards the 4.5 V input regulation threshold.
- Only DC currents are solved in the copper. Ripple currents in the capacitor loops and their ESR losses are small and stay inside the converter footprints.
- Bench verification: thermocouple or IR camera on U4, U6, L1, L2 and the pack at the charging case with the real enclosure, and at the 3.1 V / 0.8 A battery case.
