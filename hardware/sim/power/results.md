## TPS61088 boost loop

Small-signal model from the data sheet: constant off-time, peak current control, Rsense 0.08 Ω, GEA 190 µA/V. Fitted compensation R18 6.8 kΩ, C21 10 nF, C22 100 pF, with L2 2.2 µH. VOUT 5.06 V, switching frequency 0.71 to 0.73 MHz over 3.1 to 4.4 V (R16 220 kΩ). The error amplifier output resistance is not specified. 10 MΩ is assumed; it sets only the DC gain.

MODE is tied to GND (forced PWM), so the converter stays in continuous conduction at every load. The CCM model therefore applies down to the 0.05 A light-load case. Not in the model: the current-loop sampling poles at fSW/2 (a few degrees at these crossovers), slope compensation and the error amplifier bandwidth.

## Undervoltage cut-off, rebound and restart (ngspice, power/undervoltage.cir)
U11/U12 use the maximum diode-drop corner and the same separate module branches as the transient bench. Module readiness requires both terminals above 4.75 V.

The battery is an open-circuit voltage behind the pack resistance and the harness. Then follow the MAX77757 BATT-to-SYS switch (20 mΩ) and the board copper (12 mΩ from the routed tracks, J3 to L2). The TLV840 on VSYS (VIT− 2.9 V, hysteresis 72 mV, 145 mV, 203 mV, 30 µs propagation, 40 µs reset delay with CT open) pulls PWR_EN low against R13 100 kΩ. R13 is fed from VSYS through R43 1 kΩ, the closed thermostat and the closed slider. The boost, load switch and modules are those of the transient bench. The open-circuit voltage ramps from 3.30 to 2.95 V in 400 ms, holds, then ramps back to 3.30 V (restore). The ramp stands for a discharge; it is fast only against the electrical time constants. The thermal bench holds the supervisor released; this one does not.

- The cycling is set by the drop I·R across pack, harness and switch against the fixed 5 % hysteresis. The reset delay (CT, 6.2 ms per 10 nF) only stretches the off time of each cycle. Every trip drops the rail through the 230 Ω discharge, so each cycle is a module reboot. The pack's own protection cuts at 2.5 V ± 0.1 V, below all of this.
