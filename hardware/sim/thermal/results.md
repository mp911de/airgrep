## Thermostat closed: Q2 leakage into R12

With the thermostat closed Q2 is off and only R12 holds the Q6 gate (CHG_DIS) low. Q2 off-state leakage raises that gate. The AO3401A data sheet allows 1 µA at 25 °C and 5 µA at 55 °C at VDS = −30 V.
