## Thermostat closed: Q2 leakage into R12 and the CE pin

With the thermostat closed Q2 is off and only R12 (100 kΩ) holds CE (CHG_DIS) low. The AO3401A data sheet allows 1 µA at 25 °C and 5 µA at 55 °C at VDS = −30 V; at the 3 to 4.4 V here it is normally far lower. CE must stay below VIL 0.4 V, charging is disabled above VIH 1.3 V.

| Q2 IDSS | CE (CHG_DIS) | State |
|---|---|---|
| 0.1 µA | 0.009 V | below VIL |
| 0.5 µA | 0.046 V | below VIL |
| 1.0 µA | 0.093 V | below VIL |
| 5.0 µA | 0.464 V | undefined |

- PASS: CE at most 0.093 V with Q2 leakage at the 25 °C data sheet maximum, below VIL 0.4 V; the 55 °C maximum (5 µA, 0.5 V) would disable charging
