Overview
The XIAO 1.47'' IPS Touch Display(ESP32-S3) integrates a 1.47-inch color touch display,
microphone, IMU, battery, and buttons around the XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus for Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
connectivity. It is designed for small connected AIoT devices and supports applications such as
mini desktop dashboards, smart home touch controllers, AI voice assistant prototypes, and
interactive toys.

Key Features
A Mini Smart Device Board, Ready for Your Next Project
For many small hardware projects, the hard part is not starting the controller. It is turning a screen, touch input,
microphone, sensors, battery, and buttons into a small device that feels complete and usable. This board already
brings these key parts together, so you can spend more time building the actual experience: designing the UI,
reading data, adding interaction, and connecting to the network, instead of wiring modules one by one.
Make Your Projects That Look and Feel More Finished
The 1.47-inch color touch display helps your project move beyond serial output, LEDs, or rough test setups. You can
build menus, status pages, icons, buttons, sensor dashboards, and simple animations, making your prototype
easier to show, use, and share. For demos, customer presentations, classroom projects, and maker builds, it helps
the ﬁnal result feel much more complete.
Touch, Voice, and Motion in One Small Board
This board does more than show information. It also gives your project multiple ways to respond to the user. The
touch screen is great for menus and controls. The microphone can be used for recording, sound triggers, or voice
interaction. The IMU can detect orientation, shaking, ﬂipping, and motion. With these built in, you can build a small
device that can “listen”, “move”, and respond to touch, not just display data.
Built for Connected AIoT Ideas
Powered by XIAO ESP32-S3 Plus, this board is a strong ﬁt for small devices that need Wi-Fi and Bluetooth
connectivity. Build a desktop weather screen, server status monitor, Home Assistant panel, smart home touch
controller, cloud data display, or a mini AIoT node that connects sensors with online services.
