466 Series 1206 Fast-Acting Fuse                                                                                                        RoHS Pb




                                                                                                    Description
                                                                                                    The 466 Series Fast-Acting Surface Mount Fuse (SMF)
                                                                                                    is a small (1206 size) thin-film device designed for
                                                                                                    secondary protection of circuits used in space constrained
                                                                                                    applications such as hand-held portable electronic devices.
                                                                                                    This series is 100% lead-free and meets the requirements
                                                                                                    of the RoHS directive. New Halogen-Free 466 Series
                                                                                                    fuses are available to order using the “HF” suffix. See Part
                                                                                                    Numbering section for additional information.
